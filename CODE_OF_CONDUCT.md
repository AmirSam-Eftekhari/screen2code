# Code of Conduct

We want Screen2Code to be a welcoming project. Contributors and maintainers agree to: be respectful and constructive; give and accept critique of work, not people; avoid harassment,
discrimination, and personal attacks; respect privacy (never post other people's screenshots, data, or personal information without consent); and assume good faith while staying accountable for mistakes.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation. Report concerns through the private channel described
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (maintainers must enable and monitor it before the repository is made public).
